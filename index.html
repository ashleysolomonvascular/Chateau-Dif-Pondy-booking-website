Property Details

Please read through these details and watch the full video tour before booking — together they cover everything you need to know before you arrive.

  • Located about 15 km from Pondy Rock Beach and 10 km from Auroville Junction.
  • Self-check-in, self-service stay.
  • Non-air-conditioned — the ventilated Italian roof tiles mean AC isn't possible — but well-ventilated with ceiling fans and tower fans for comfort.
  • On windy nights, a little dust may enter through small roof gaps. We minimize this and keep the beds protected with dust covers.
  • Guests are welcome to enjoy drinks anywhere in the house, while smoking is restricted to the outdoors.
  • Have a question we haven't covered? Message us on WhatsApp any time.
